# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## About the Series

**Stranger Things** is a science fiction and horror television series created by *The Duffer Brothers*. It follows a group of friends who discover mysterious events and supernatural creatures in their small town.

## Why I Like It

I like this series because it combines mystery, adventure, friendship, and suspense. Every episode makes me curious about what will happen next. I also enjoy how the characters support one another despite the challenges they face.

> "Friends don't lie."
